About me

I am a Ph.D. student at The University of Tokyo, working with Prof. Hideya Ochiai in the Esaki & Ochiai Lab.

My research focuses on multimodal federated learning, with an emphasis on missing modalities and heterogeneous clients. I also work on multimodal understanding and LLM-driven smart contract security.

Previously, I received my M.Eng. from Beijing Institute of Technology, advised by Prof. Keke Gai, and my B.Eng. and B.Econ. from Jilin University. I was a research assistant with Prof. Zitong Yu at Great Bay University and a visiting student with Prof. Giulia Boato at the University of Trento.
